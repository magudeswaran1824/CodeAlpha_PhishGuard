LEARN THE BASICS

Understanding Phishing

Phishing is a cyber attack in which attackers impersonate trusted organizations or people to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

📧

Email Phishing

Fraudulent emails designed to deceive recipients.

📱

SMS Phishing

Suspicious text messages used to trick mobile users.

🌐

Website Phishing

Fake websites designed to imitate legitimate services.

IDENTIFY THE THREATS

Recognize a Phishing Attempt

⚠️ Sample Suspicious Email

Subject: Urgent! Your account needs verification.

Message: Your account may be suspended. Please verify your information immediately.

Training example only — this is not a real email.

Common Warning Signs

🚨 Urgent Requests

Messages that pressure you to act immediately.

🔗 Suspicious Links

Unexpected links or misleading website addresses.

🔐 Sensitive Information

Unexpected requests for passwords or OTPs.

How to Stay Protected

  • Verify the sender's email address.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Avoid unexpected links and attachments.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Report suspicious messages.
